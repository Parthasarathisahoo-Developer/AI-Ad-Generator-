Simple ways to make your ad copy better

Use these practical principles when reviewing the advertising copy generated for your campaign.

01. Start with the customer

Explain what the customer gets from your product instead of only describing what your business does.

02. Highlight the real benefit

Focus on a meaningful result, convenience, saving, feature or experience that makes your offer useful.

03. Choose relevant search terms

Use keywords that actually describe your product and match what potential customers are likely to search for.

04. Keep the message clear

Avoid unnecessary words and exaggerated claims. A clear message is easier to understand and evaluate.

05. Test different versions

Compare different headlines and descriptions to discover which message connects best with your audience.
